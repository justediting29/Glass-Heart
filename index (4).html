Glass Heart

Some things break quietly.

In a city that never learned how to keep its promises, Mara collects the things people leave behind: letters, half-finished songs, and the small truths they were too afraid to say aloud. When a familiar name appears in a stranger's handwriting, the past begins asking to be let in.
